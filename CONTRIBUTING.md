# Contributing to Semantic SEO Geek

Issues and suggestions are welcome. Version 1 does not accept external code, documentation, skill, or other content contributions.

This temporary boundary keeps the project's provenance guarantees simple to maintain: every published line remains the work of one author writing from the public sources listed in the source register. Do not open a pull request: it will be closed without merging, even if the proposed change is useful.

## Report a bug

Use the bug-report issue form. Include:

- the installed version;
- the agent environment and version;
- a minimal reproduction;
- the expected and observed behavior; and
- the relevant validation output with secrets and private data removed.

## Suggest a workflow improvement

Use the workflow-request issue form. Describe the user problem, the desired output, and any current primary documentation that supports a changing platform claim.

Do not submit classroom materials, publication text, transcripts, paid resources, private research, copied examples, generated summaries of non-public sources, credentials, customer data, or personal notes.

## Security reports

Do not report an unpatched vulnerability or accidental private-data exposure in a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Future contribution policy

External contributions may open after the maintainer adopts a lawyer-reviewed contributor agreement and updates this file. Until then, suggestions do not grant permission to copy third-party material, and submitting an issue does not promise implementation.
