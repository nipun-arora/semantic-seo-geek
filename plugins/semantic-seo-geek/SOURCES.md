## Interpretation boundaries

- The workflows translate public guidance into repeatable professional practice; they do not claim that every workflow step is a ranking requirement.
- Valid structured data can establish eligibility for a supported feature, not guaranteed display.
- No listed source establishes a universal content length, keyword density, SEO score, required schema type, ranking guarantee, or reliable authorship inference from prose patterns.
- The bundled shell scripts perform narrow mechanical checks. They do not replace rendered-page, accessibility, factual, legal, or platform validation.
