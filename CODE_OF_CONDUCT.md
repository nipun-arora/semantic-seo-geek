# Community conduct

Semantic SEO Geek's public issue tracker is for clear, respectful collaboration about the project.

## Participate constructively

- Discuss the work, evidence, and impact—not a person's identity or motives.
- Make disagreement specific enough that another person can evaluate it.
- Correct mistakes openly and without retaliation.
- Protect private information, credentials, customer data, and licensed third-party works.
- Follow the repository's contribution, security, and licensing boundaries.

## Conduct that is not accepted

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Knowingly submitting proprietary, confidential, plagiarized, or unlicensed material.
- Impersonation, false claims of endorsement, spam, or sustained disruption.
- Pressuring someone to disclose protected or private third-party works.

## Reporting and response

For an ordinary issue-thread problem, stop engaging and contact Nipun Arora through a public contact method on <https://github.com/nipun-arora>. Ask for a private channel before sharing sensitive details.

The maintainer may hide or remove content, lock a discussion, reject a submission, restrict participation, or ban an account. Decisions consider severity, repetition, context, evidence, and the safety of affected people. Illegal threats or credible safety risks may be escalated to the platform or appropriate authorities.

This policy applies to the repository, its issue tracker, and project spaces controlled by the maintainer.
